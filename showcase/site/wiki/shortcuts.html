Rebinding

Open Settings › Shortcuts and input. Search by action or by current binding, or filter by context, customised bindings, conflicts or unassigned actions.

  1. Click a binding to replace it, or Add for an alternative.
  2. Press the key or mouse button with its modifiers. Keys follow your layout by default; physical mode follows key position instead.
  3. Save binding. If another action in an overlapping context uses the same input, choose another or replace that assignment explicitly.

Disable removes an action's bindings, Reset restores its defaults and Restore all defaults resets everything. Primary, middle, secondary, back and forward mouse buttons can be bound when your mouse delivers them to the app; Test input shows what your device sends. Bindings are saved per device and apply across workspaces.
