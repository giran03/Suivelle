Canvases

Some thinking doesn't fit in rows or paragraphs. Canvases are infinite boards where cards, notes, images and whole pages can sit anywhere, at any size, joined by labelled arrows.

Two kinds of canvas

Database canvas
A view of a database. Record cards are placed freely, alongside notes and images, and stay live: change a record and its card updates.
Workspace canvas
A whiteboard for any pages. Make as many as you like from the Canvases section in the sidebar, next to the page tree controls.

Pages you can edit on the board

Drag pages from the sidebar onto a workspace canvas, or use Add page. Each becomes a panel showing the page's full blocks, with an editable title, the usual block tools and autosave. Panel bodies scroll; drag the header to move one. New panels open at a comfortable reading size, and removing a panel leaves its page untouched. Text and blocks stay sharp at every zoom level.

Notes, images and arrows

Double-click the background for a note. Paste or add images. Draw arrows between any two items and give them labels, so a canvas can be a flowchart, a story map or a system diagram. Everything moves, resizes and connects.

Each canvas remembers where you left it.

Arranging

Drag across the background to select several items, then align them, space them evenly, restack or duplicate them. Alignment guides appear as you drag. Ctrl Z and Ctrl Shift Z undo and redo, and right-click opens a menu for items and for the background.

Keeping canvases

Canvases can be renamed, duplicated and deleted. Workspace canvases are included in workspace exports, and duplicating a database canvas view copies its layout.
