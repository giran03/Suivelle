# Suivelle 0.1.8

Unreleased.

## Improvements

- Pages everywhere (main view, split panes, the peek panel and narrow windows) keep room on the left for the block handle, so the add and drag buttons no longer stick out of the page.
- Split panes can no longer be dragged narrower than 360 px or shorter than 280 px, so a pane never shrinks to an unreadable sliver.
- Settings pages are organised into titled sections with a one-line description and a card of rows: Layout and motion (Shape and lines, Window frame, Icons, Size and spacing, Motion), Editor (with the sample on top), Window and startup (Startup, Tray), Data and backups (Backups, Files, Storage health), Appearance (Mode, Accent) and About. Each page opens with a short note on what it is for.
- Settings scrolls by dragging: hold the mouse on empty space and drag, and the page follows with easing and a little momentum. Buttons, switches, fields and text you can select still behave as usual.
- The Updates page is restyled like Octopolis: a status card with a tone tile, tidier automatic-update rows, and a What’s new section for your version with a View changelogs button that opens every release, folded by version.
- Tooltips no longer jump between one line and several while they grow or move between buttons; the text keeps its final shape and is revealed as the bubble resizes.
- Settings > Shortcuts and input is tidier: context chips replace the dropdowns, each context is its own card with its key caps on the right, edited shortcuts are tagged, and Disable, Add and Reset appear when you hover a row.
- The command palette now opens with Ctrl+P (Ctrl+K no longer opens it). You can still rebind it in Settings > Shortcuts and input.
- The browser shortcuts built into the window (print, save page, view source, reload, find next, caret browsing and zoom keys) are switched off, so none of them can pop up over the app.
- The palette matches fuzzily ("swtch" finds "Switch to dark theme") and puts what you use most first, for commands and pages.
- Layout and motion adds Icon style (Outlined, Rounded, Sharp), Icon weight and Icon fill.
- Holding a dragged page over a tab for a moment fills the tab with a bar and opens it. A closed split pane shrinks and fades away.
- Dragging a tab, page or pane over a pane now works like Octopolis: every edge pulses, the edge under the pointer glows and a skeleton of the new pane shows where it will open. Split panes are separate rounded cards, the focused one outlined, and new panes grow in. Tabs use the same darker strip with a lit top edge on the current tab.
- Settings > Agents is split into five tabs (General, Agents, Usage, Memory, Files) instead of one long page, with a count of waiting proposals on General.
- Every interface icon is now a Google Material Symbol, the same set Octopolis uses.
- The whole app now uses the Octopolis look: the page, toolbar and tabs sit on one raised card over the window frame, buttons and menus use stronger outlines, and corners are slightly tighter.
- Appearance offers 17 themes grouped as Light, Dark, High contrast, Minimal, Vibrant and Pastel, each shown as a small preview. Text and status colours in every theme are adjusted just enough to stay readable. The default is now Octopolis Midnight (dark) and Paper (light).
- The sidebar's Recent list no longer repeats pages that are already in Favorites.
- The sidebar collapse button has a larger click area.
- Board views fade the edge where more columns are waiting to be scrolled into view.
- In Settings, Appearance keeps the palette presets and preview up front and tucks the individual colour rows under "Fine-tune individual colors".
- The sidebar's Trash, Backups, Import & export and Settings buttons now sit in one compact row of icons. Hover any of them to see its name.
- Node Map and the agents Inbox now share one row at the top of the sidebar, and Node Map fills the row when there is no Inbox.
- Page icons in the sidebar and elsewhere are no longer cropped: images that aren't square are shown whole, and emoji are no longer clipped.
- Toggle switches settle smoothly instead of overshooting.
- Settings > Layout and motion now lets you switch between Windows titlebar buttons and macOS traffic lights (placed on the left or right), and the titlebar blends cleanly with the window background.
- Canvas boards now feel smoother and easier to navigate: the board title pill hugs its title over a frosted surface, cards animate in with subtle elevation and optional accent tinting, page cards can fold to their header or zoom to fit on a double-click, middle-clicking pans from anywhere (including inside embedded pages), minimap clicks glide smoothly, connector handles and edges highlight their targets and stay easy to grab when zoomed out, and the dot grid tracks your pan and zoom.
- Right-clicking the tray icon now opens a lightweight native menu instantly without using extra background memory, synced live with your theme, accent colour, spacing, corner style and display scaling, and positioned next to the icon away from the taskbar. Escape, arrow keys or clicking elsewhere navigate and dismiss it.
